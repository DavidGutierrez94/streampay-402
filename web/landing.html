Bill agents by the second, not by the request.

streampay-402 adds per-second streaming payments to x402. Agents lock a deposit in escrow, your service meters usage every second, and anything unused is refunded onchain the moment the session ends.

How it works

Three steps, all onchain.

1

Sign up

Email login via Privy creates an embedded wallet on Monad. No extension, no seed phrase.

2

Register your service

Set a price per second. The ServiceRegistry stores your terms and a hash of your secret key.

3

Get paid per second

Use Stripe-style pk_/sk_ keys. Agents hit a 402, open a stream, and settle with refunds.
